# Press / HARO templates for Implenix

Use these for HARO (Help A Reporter Out), Featured.com, Qwoted, Terkel,
SourceBottle, and direct press pitches. Goal: earn backlinks from
authoritative news/business publications, which is the single highest-
leverage off-site SEO lever.

Three templates by use case. Personalize the first paragraph each
time — generic boilerplate is the most common HARO failure mode.

topic.

## What NOT to do in HARO responses

Common failures (most pitches get ignored for one of these):

1. **Generic boilerplate.** Reporters scan the first 2 sentences. If
   you don't address their specific question in the first sentence,
   you're out.
2. **Marketing copy.** "Implenix is the leading AI receptionist
   platform" gets ignored. "Our HVAC portfolio shows X% capture rate"
   gets quoted.
3. **No specific numbers.** Reporters need quotable specifics. Always
   include at least one percentage, dollar number, or time interval.
4. **Pitching the wrong story.** If the reporter is writing about
   ethics, don't pitch ROI. Match their angle exactly.
5. **Asking them to "schedule a call."** They are on deadline. Give
   them the quote in the email; offer the call only if they want
   depth.
6. **Forgetting the source URL.** Always include a relevant link to
   the Implenix site so they have something to attribute.

## Outlets to prioritize

In rough order of SEO value (Domain Authority + relevance to our
target audience):

1. **Inc.com, Forbes.com, Entrepreneur.com** — high DA, SMB audience
2. **TechCrunch, VentureBeat** — high DA, AI category coverage
3. **Local-services trade press** — ACHRNews (HVAC), Dentistry IQ
   (dental), Inman (real estate), Above the Law (legal),
   Medesthetics (med spa)
4. **Business2Community, Hackernoon** — medium DA, easy placement
5. **Reddit AMAs** — r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, specific
   industry subs — not strictly "press" but drive direct traffic and
   sometimes get cited
