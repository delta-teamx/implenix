Financial services has a specific call-handling problem most categories
don't share: the conversations are simultaneously high-value and highly
regulated. The advisor or mortgage broker who closes the most deals is
the one with the longest wait time on inbound first-touch — and that
gap is where AI agents earn their seat.

## Who this is for

Financial-services operators — solo advisors through ~50-seat
operations — running an inbound intake problem. Mortgage brokerages,
RIAs, insurance agencies, accounting firms, and CPA practices all fit
this shape.

5. **Consultation booking** — books advisor calendars honoring block
   schedules, market-hours rules, and consultation-type duration.
6. **Follow-up sequence** — multi-touch nurture on prospective
   clients who do not book the first call (consent-driven, compliant
   with your jurisdiction).
7. **Existing-client routing** — recognizes returning clients and
   routes directly to their assigned advisor.

## What the AI does NOT do (and why this matters in finance)

This list is the difference between a deployable financial AI agent
and a regulatory disaster:

- **Provide financial advice.** No suitability assessments, no product
  recommendations, no "you should consider X." Captures intake, books
  the advisor.
- **Quote specific rates.** Mortgage rates, insurance premiums, advisory
  fees — all routed to humans. The AI confirms range bands but never
  commits a specific number.
- **Discuss client account specifics.** Existing clients calling about
  balance, trades, or transactions get routed to a human with proper
  verification flow.
- **Sell products.** The entire FINRA / state-securities rule set
  around suitability requires a licensed human. AI never crosses
  this line.
- **Engage with senior-vulnerable or distressed callers.** Sentiment
  rules route these to humans within 30 seconds.
- **Confirm pre-approvals or commitments.** "Will I qualify" questions
  route to a human underwriter.

<Pull>The line in financial services: AI captures and books. Humans advise, commit, and sell. Anything that crosses into "advice" is a hard transfer rule.</Pull>

## Use cases by sub-vertical

### Mortgage brokers

Highest-volume financial AI use case. Inbound qualification (purchase
vs refinance, property type, target loan amount, credit posture,
employment status, timeline) captured to LOS, qualified leads booked
into loan officer calendars. Lifts qualified-app volume by 25-35% on
typical deployments.

### Financial advisors / RIAs

Lower-volume but high-LTV. AI handles inbound from referrals,
seminars, and content. Captures basic suitability data (target
relationship size, current situation, timeline) and books the
discovery call. Advisor's time concentrates on conversations that
have real conversion potential.

### Insurance agencies

Mixed inbound — new quote requests, renewal questions, claims
intake. AI handles new-quote intake completely (driver/property/health
info as relevant), routes renewals to the assigned agent, routes
claims to the dedicated claims line. Strong fit for independent
agencies running 3-15 producers.

### Accounting / CPA firms

Tax-season-driven. AI absorbs the storm of new-client inquiries from
Jan-April, books consultations against partner calendars, manages
document collection. Without the AI, partners spend 60% of season on
intake instead of returns.

## Compliance framework

Every Implenix financial deployment runs within four explicit
guardrails:

1. **No advice, ever.** Hard-coded in the script. The agent declines
   any "should I" or "what would you recommend" question.
2. **Data handling per Reg compliance** — SOC 2 controls,
   encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, audit
   logs. SBA / FINRA / SEC examination-ready.
3. **Recording disclosure** — every call discloses recording per
   state two-party-consent rules.
4. **Vulnerable-caller flags** — sentiment + lexical detection routes
   distressed, confused, or elderly callers to human handlers with
   documented escalation.

## What deployment looks like

Financial deployments take 16-24 business days (longer than other
verticals due to compliance review):

1. **Discovery + compliance scoping** (3-4 days)
2. **Build + script tuning** (5-8 days)
3. **Compliance review** with your CCO (3-5 days)
4. **Parallel run** (5-7 days)
5. **Cutover** with continued recording review
