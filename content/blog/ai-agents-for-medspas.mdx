Med spa inbound is a booking-density problem. The aesthetician is in
the room, the front desk is on another call, the would-be-new-patient
is browsing on her phone at 9pm and taps "call." Most med spa
operators are losing the bulk of their new-patient pipeline to that
3-way collision. AI voice agents solve it.

## Who this is for

Med spa operators — single-location through small chain — with
appointment-density inbound. The math gets sharper for operators
already running paid social or paid search, because the new-patient
inquiries are time-sensitive.

The workflow that ships:

1. **24/7 inbound** — captures the evening and weekend bookings that
   account for 40-60% of new patient inquiries.
2. **Service-specific intake** — Botox, filler, laser, body
   contouring, skincare each follow their own qualifying questions.
   Captures medical history flags that need provider review.
3. **Provider matching** — books with the right injector for the
   service requested.
4. **New-patient consultation booking** — books an in-person consult
   for first-time aesthetic procedures.
5. **Package nurture sequence** — multi-touch outreach to prospects
   inquiring about packages (10-session laser, body contouring
   series) who do not book the first call.
6. **Pre-appointment reminders** — two-touch (48hr SMS + 24hr voice)
   reduces no-show rate by 29% on typical deployments.
7. **Post-treatment follow-up** — 48-hour SMS check-in, retention
   scheduling at the appropriate interval (Botox 12 weeks, filler
   3-6 months).
8. **Retention re-engagement** — automated outreach to clients past
   their typical return window.

## The math that drives the ROI

Med spa LTV runs $1,200-$2,500 for the typical client across their
relationship. Three things compound:

1. **New-patient capture lift** — typical +34% from after-hours
   booking
2. **Retention improvement** — typical +15% from systematic re-
   engagement sequence
3. **Front-desk hours reclaimed** — 8-12 hours per FTE per week from
   automated booking

A 2-location med spa with $1,800 average client LTV doing 320
new-patient inquiries per month sees:

- Pre-deployment monthly missed pipeline: ~$48,000
- Post-deployment captured pipeline: ~$38,800
- Plus retention lift: ~$12,000/month in re-booked patients
- AI cost: $397-$497/month

Payback window: typically 8-13 days.

## What the AI does NOT do

Honest boundaries:

- **Medical advice.** The AI does not opine on whether you should
  get a procedure, how it will work for your skin type, or whether
  you are a candidate. Routes to the provider for consultation.
- **Pricing for complex packages.** Custom-quote packages route to a
  human consultation.
- **Medical-history-flag handling.** Any flag in the intake (active
  acne medication, pregnancy, blood thinners, recent surgery) routes
  to a provider review before any booking.
- **Cancellations or refund discussions.** Always to a human.
- **Aesthetic preference discussions.** "What would look best on me"
  is always a human consultation.

## Integrations that matter

- **Boulevard** — direct API for booking and customer record
  enrichment
- **Vagaro** — supported with two-way sync
- **Mindbody** — supported
- **Aesthetic Record** — for medical-grade aesthetic practices
- **GoHighLevel** — for the multi-touch follow-up and retention
  sequences
- **Twilio** — for SMS confirmations and reminders

## What deployment looks like

Med spa deployments run 12-18 business days:

1. **Discovery (2-3 days):** Service mix review, medical-history-flag
   rules, provider scheduling rules, integration scope.
2. **Build (5-7 days):** Service-specific intake scripts, provider
   matching, calendar booking against Boulevard/Vagaro/Mindbody,
   medical-flag routing.
3. **Provider review (2-3 days):** Medical director reviews script
   and approves flag-routing logic.
4. **Parallel run (5-7 days):** Live alongside existing front desk,
   tuning, edge case handling.
5. **Cutover.** Production traffic flips with continued recording
   review.
