If you're on the vendor side of a B2B enterprise sale, security
questionnaires eat weeks of engineering time. If you're on the buyer
side, sending them is a compliance necessity that nobody enjoys. AI
agents that answer or generate these questionnaires have become the
category's most-adopted operational tool in the last 18 months.

This is the honest 2026 comparison of the players.

## Who this is for

- **Vendor side:** Security engineers, GRC leads, and revenue
  operations at companies selling to enterprise buyers who send you
  questionnaires (SIG, CAIQ, VSAQ, custom)
- **Buyer side:** Security teams sending questionnaires to vendors
  and drowning in vendor-answered PDFs

The AI agent categories differ between vendor and buyer use — cover
both below.

The four vendors dominating this space:

### 1. Loopio

Longest-running dedicated response management platform. AI questionnaire
completion added ~2023, matured through 2025. Widely deployed at enterprise
scale.

- **Best for:** Enterprises with 200+ questionnaires per year and complex
  content library management
- **Weaknesses:** Enterprise pricing, learning curve, feels heavy for
  smaller teams
- **Notable customer segments:** SaaS platform companies, large services
  firms

### 2. SafeBase

Emerged 2020, aggressive push into AI questionnaire response in 2024-25.
Two-sided: proactive trust center (buyers self-serve most questions)
plus AI agent for the questionnaires that still come through.

- **Best for:** Companies where a large % of questionnaires can be
  deflected by a well-maintained trust center
- **Weaknesses:** Trust center adoption requires buyer education
- **Notable:** Reduces total questionnaire volume, not just per-question
  time

### 3. Vanta

Started as SOC 2 automation, expanded into AI-driven questionnaire
response. Strong integration with existing compliance evidence.

- **Best for:** Companies already using Vanta for SOC 2/ISO/HIPAA
  compliance
- **Weaknesses:** If you're not on Vanta for compliance, standalone AI
  questionnaire feature is not the best
- **Notable:** Answers stay in sync with compliance evidence
  automatically

### 4. Drata

Similar shape to Vanta. AI questionnaire response layered on top of
compliance automation platform.

- **Best for:** Same profile as Vanta, competitive on price
- **Weaknesses:** AI questionnaire capabilities lag Loopio and SafeBase
  slightly (still shipping fast)

## Buyer-side: AI agents that review vendor responses

The category most people forget exists but is genuinely useful:

### 1. Whistic

Buyer-side questionnaire management with AI review of vendor
responses. Flags gaps, contradictions, and risk indicators
automatically.

- **Best for:** Security teams reviewing 100+ vendors per year
- **Notable:** Reduces manual review time by 60-80%

### 2. UpGuard

TPRM (third-party risk management) with AI-augmented vendor review.
Broader scope than just questionnaires.

- **Best for:** Enterprise programs with continuous vendor monitoring
- **Notable:** AI questionnaire review is one feature; not the primary
  product

### 3. Panorays

Similar TPRM shape. AI questionnaire review as part of broader
vendor risk platform.

## What the AI agent actually does

Under the hood, the AI questionnaire response workflow:

1. **Ingest incoming questionnaire** (PDF, Excel, native web form)
2. **Parse into structured questions** with metadata (question type,
   required, category)
3. **Retrieve relevant answers** from your content library, past
   questionnaires, compliance evidence, and policy documents
4. **Draft response** grounded in the retrieved evidence
5. **Flag questions requiring human review** (novel questions, high-
   sensitivity, contradictions with prior answers)
6. **Human reviews and approves** — usually 10-20% of questions
7. **Push completed response back** to the requesting party

The RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) architecture is critical. AI
that "makes up" security answers is a compliance disaster. Every
answer must trace to source evidence.

## What to look for in an AI questionnaire agent

Six criteria:

1. **Evidence traceability.** Every AI-generated answer links to the
   source content it came from.
2. **Content library maturity.** How well does the tool manage,
   version, and expire your prior answers.
3. **Confidence scoring.** Does the AI tell you which answers it's
   sure about vs which need human review?
4. **Compliance evidence integration.** Can it read your SOC 2, ISO
   27001, HIPAA evidence and use it as answer source material?
5. **Custom questionnaire handling.** Standard formats (SIG, CAIQ) are
   easy; enterprise-custom questionnaires are harder.
6. **Review workflow.** How does it route flagged questions to the
   right SME for review?

## What NOT to use

Generic LLM chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) fed your security
policies and asked to answer questionnaires. Three reasons this fails:

1. **No evidence traceability.** You can't audit the AI's reasoning.
2. **Hallucination risk.** LLM makes up plausible-sounding answers to
   questions your policies don't actually address.
3. **No content management.** You have no versioned library, no
   answer expiration, no change tracking.

The dedicated tools solve all three. Use them.
