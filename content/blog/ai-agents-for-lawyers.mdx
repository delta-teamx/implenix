Law firms have a specific operational problem: the partners who close
the most business are the same people who cannot answer the phone.
Court, depositions, client meetings — the high-billing-rate work is
incompatible with first-call response. AI voice agents are a clean
fit for the layer between the inbound call and the partner.

This is the operator-level guide for solo practitioners through
mid-sized firms.

## Who this is for

Law firms — solo through ~25 attorneys — running an inbound intake
problem. If you are 50+ attorneys with a dedicated intake department,
the math still works but the scoping is different.

1. **24/7 inbound answering** — every call picked up regardless of
   attorney availability.
2. **Practice-area triage** — "what kind of legal matter are you
   calling about?" routes to the correct intake script (PI, family,
   estate, criminal, business).
3. **Conflict check intake** — caller name, opposing party, basic
   matter summary. Captured to your conflict-check system before any
   substantive conversation.
4. **Jurisdiction qualification** — confirms the matter is in your
   practice jurisdiction before booking.
5. **Consultation booking** — books the attorney's calendar honoring
   block schedules, court calendar, and consultation-type duration
   rules (free 15-min vs paid 30-min).
6. **Intake documentation** — captures matter description, timeline,
   contact info, source of referral. Writes to your CMS (Clio,
   MyCase, PracticePanther) during the call.
7. **Follow-up sequence** — multi-touch nurture on prospective
   clients who do not book the first call.

## What the AI does NOT do

This list matters more for law firms than for any other vertical.

- **Provide legal advice.** Every Implenix legal deployment includes
  an explicit "I cannot provide legal advice" guardrail in the
  script. The agent collects intake and books consultations — it
  does not opine on the matter.
- **Discuss confidentiality.** The agent confirms the prospective
  attorney-client privilege framework and routes substantive
  conversations to the attorney.
- **Run discovery or document review.** AI document review is a
  separate category (Harvey, Casetext, etc.) — not what we do.
- **Handle calls from existing clients about active matters.**
  Existing clients with active case numbers route directly to the
  attorney or paralegal handling the matter.
- **Engage in fee discussions.** Pricing conversations route to a
  human.

## The ethical framework that makes it work

Every legal AI deployment we ship operates within four explicit
boundaries:

1. **No legal advice.** Hard-coded in the script. The agent declines
   any "what should I do" question and routes to an attorney.
2. **No attorney-client privilege creation.** The agent explicitly
   states it cannot establish privilege; substantive discussion is
   tagged for attorney follow-up.
3. **No confidentiality promises.** The agent collects intake under
   the firm's standard data-handling policy, which is disclosed at
   start of call.
4. **No engagement decisions.** The agent does not accept or decline
   representation. Conflict check and qualification happen; the
   attorney decides whether to take the matter.

These guardrails are not optional — they are how legal AI stays on
the right side of state bar ethics rules.

## Use cases by practice area

### Personal injury

Highest-volume legal use case. Inbound qualification (mechanism of
injury, timing, fault, insurance status, medical treatment received,
prior PI matters) captured to CMS, qualified leads booked into the
intake attorney's calendar same day. Lifts qualified-consult volume
by 30-50% on typical deployments.

### Family law

Sensitive but high-volume. Intake (case type, jurisdiction, urgency,
custody/finance/divorce-only) captured. Crisis-flag rules route
genuinely emotional calls to human intake immediately. Booking
conversations qualified for the consultation-type rate (initial,
emergency, return).

### Estate planning

Lower volume but high LTV. Intake (existing estate documents, family
structure, business interests, special-needs considerations) captured
to CMS. Educational follow-up sequence runs for non-booking
prospects, often re-engages them 6-12 months later when the
"someday" finally happens.

### Business / corporate

Often referral-driven. AI confirms referral source, captures matter
type (entity formation, contracts, M&A, disputes), books partner
consultation directly. Triages routine vs strategic matters.

### Criminal defense

24/7 coverage is the entire value proposition. After-hours arrest
calls are time-sensitive — AI captures the case posture, court
appearance dates, and contact info, then routes to the on-call
attorney via SIP transfer.

## Integrations that matter

- **Clio** — direct API for intake write-back, contact creation,
  matter setup
- **MyCase** — full integration including custom intake forms
- **PracticePanther** — supported with two-way sync
- **Lawmatics** — for firms running marketing-automation alongside CMS
- **Google Calendar / Outlook** — for attorney calendar booking
- **Calendly** — for consultation scheduling layer

## What deployment looks like

Legal deployments take 14-21 business days (longer than other
verticals because the script tuning around the ethical guardrails
requires careful review):

1. **Discovery (2-3 days):** Practice-area mix review, ethical
   guardrails alignment, conflict-check process, CMS integration
   scope.
2. **Build (5-8 days):** Practice-area-specific intake scripts,
   ethical guardrail testing, integration setup, calendar rules.
3. **Attorney review (3-5 days):** Senior attorney reviews the full
   script and approves the guardrails before live deployment.
4. **Parallel run (5-7 days):** Live alongside existing intake,
   recordings reviewed, edge cases handled.
5. **Cutover.** Production traffic flips with continued recording
   review for the first two weeks.
