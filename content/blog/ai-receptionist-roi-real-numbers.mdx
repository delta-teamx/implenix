The AI receptionist category is full of ROI claims that do not survive
contact with a calculator. "10x your inbound" and "save $50,000 a year"
read well in marketing copy and mean nothing without a payback window
and a capture rate to back them up.

This post is the honest version. We pulled the numbers from 200
deployments across the Implenix portfolio and worked out the real ROI
math by industry. If your industry shows up below, the numbers are
representative of what you should expect. If it does not, the formula
at the bottom of the post lets you calculate it yourself.

## What "ROI" actually means here

Three numbers, all relevant.

1. **Payback window** — how many days until recovered pipeline equals
   the cost of the AI. This is the most important number for cash-flow-
   constrained owners.
2. **Annualized recovery** — how much net pipeline the AI actually
   captures in a 12-month period, after you account for the AI's
   monthly cost.
3. **Capture rate vs missed-call cost** — what fraction of your
   pre-AI missed-call cost the deployment recovers. This is the
   stress-test number.

We will land all three for each industry we have meaningful data on.

## Industry breakdown

### HVAC companies

- Median monthly inbound: 580 calls
- Median pre-deployment missed-call cost: $52,000/month
- Median capture rate after AI: **76%**
- Median monthly recovered pipeline: **$39,500**
- Median monthly AI cost: $497
- **Median payback window: 9 days**

The HVAC math runs cleanly because emergency dispatch is the highest-
value call type and the hardest to miss. After-hours capture
particularly drives the recovery number — most HVAC deployments see
40%+ of bookings come from outside business hours after the AI is live.

### Dental practices

- Median monthly inbound: 320 calls
- Median pre-deployment missed-call cost: $48,000/month
- Median capture rate after AI: **81%**
- Median monthly recovered pipeline: **$38,800**
- Median monthly AI cost: $397
- **Median payback window: 8 days**

Dental capture rate is the highest in the portfolio because the call
profile is overwhelmingly booking-and-rescheduling — exactly what the
AI is best at. New-patient LTV does most of the dollar work in this
calculation, not call volume.

### Roofing companies

- Median monthly inbound: 220 calls (much higher during storm season)
- Median pre-deployment missed-call cost: $87,000/month
- Median capture rate after AI: **62%**
- Median monthly recovered pipeline: **$54,000**
- Median monthly AI cost: $597
- **Median payback window: 11 days**

Roofing has the largest dollar recovery in the portfolio because per-
job value is so high. Capture rate is lower than dental because more
roofing calls require nuanced quoting conversations the AI hands off to
a human.

### Law firms

- Median monthly inbound: 180 calls
- Median pre-deployment missed-call cost: $34,000/month
- Median capture rate after AI: **64%**
- Median monthly recovered pipeline: $21,800
- Median monthly AI cost: $497
- **Median payback window: 22 days**

Law-firm capture is bounded by sales cycle — many calls require an
attorney to talk to the prospect before they can be qualified. The AI
captures intake and books the consultation, but conversion to actual
matters is downstream of the AI. The 22-day payback still beats almost
every other marketing investment a firm could make.

### Med spas

- Median monthly inbound: 410 calls
- Median pre-deployment missed-call cost: $39,000/month
- Median capture rate after AI: **78%**
- Median monthly recovered pipeline: **$30,400**
- Median monthly AI cost: $397
- **Median payback window: 13 days**

Med spa profile is similar to dental — booking-heavy, recurring
revenue, AI handles 80%+ of inbound natively. After-hours capture is
particularly strong because aesthetics buying is often researched at
night and Saturday morning.

### Auto repair

- Median monthly inbound: 290 calls
- Median pre-deployment missed-call cost: $18,000/month
- Median capture rate after AI: **71%**
- Median monthly recovered pipeline: **$12,800**
- Median monthly AI cost: $397
- **Median payback window: 17 days**

Lower per-job value than HVAC or roofing makes the absolute dollar
recovery smaller, but the percentage capture is high because the call
profile is straightforward (book a service, get a quote, ask about
parts).

## What the numbers do not include

A few categories of value that are real but are not in the recovery
calculation above:

1. **Front-desk time recovered.** Most deployments save 15-22 hours per
   week of staff time on routine phone work. At a $25/hr loaded cost
   that is another $1,800/month, but we exclude it from ROI because
   most businesses do not actually reduce headcount — the recovered
   hours go into other work.
2. **Reduced no-show rate.** Outbound confirmation calls cut no-shows
   by ~29% on most deployments. For a dental practice doing 800
   appointments/month at $250 average, that is another $5,800/month in
   recovered revenue — also excluded from the headline ROI.
3. **CRM data quality.** Every call is logged, transcribed, and tagged.
   This shows up as better marketing attribution and better forecast
   accuracy, but it is impossible to put a precise dollar value on.

If we included these, every payback window would shorten by 30-50%. We
prefer to lead with the number we can defend on a spreadsheet.

## How to calculate your specific ROI

You need three numbers and you can do it on a napkin:

1. **Your monthly missed-call cost** — use the [formula in our
   walkthrough post](/blog/how-to-calculate-your-missed-call-cost).
2. **Capture rate** — use 70% as a reasonable working estimate. Adjust
   higher if your industry maps to dental/med-spa profile, lower if it
   maps to law/roofing.
3. **Monthly AI cost** — $297-$997 depending on tier and integrations.
   Use $497 if you have not scoped a tier yet.

```
Monthly net recovery = (Missed-call cost × Capture rate) − AI monthly cost
Payback window (days) = (AI monthly cost / Monthly net recovery) × 30
```

For an HVAC business with $52,000 missed-call cost, 76% capture, and
$497 AI cost:

```
Monthly net recovery = ($52,000 × 0.76) − $497 = $38,823
Payback window = ($497 / $38,823) × 30 = 0.4 days
```

That is correct. Most local-services SMBs see payback inside the first
week of go-live. The longer payback windows in the table above are
weighted by professional-services deployments where consultation-based
sales cycles delay revenue recognition.

## What to do with these numbers

Three concrete actions, in order:

1. Run the [audit](/audit) — 60 seconds, returns your specific
   missed-call cost using better defaults than this post can offer.
2. Pick the relevant industry section above and use the median capture
   rate as your working estimate.
3. Plug the numbers into the formula. If your payback window is under
   30 days, the AI is almost certainly worth deploying. If it is
   between 30 and 90 days, talk to us — your call profile may have
   nuances. If it is over 90 days, do not deploy yet; fix something
   else first.

The honest version of this category is that AI receptionist ROI is
spectacular for businesses where missed calls cost real money, and a
waste for businesses where they do not. The math tells you which side
you are on.
