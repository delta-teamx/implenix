"How much does an AI agent cost" gets a hundred wrong answers on the
internet. Vendor pricing pages list a price but hide the per-minute
fees. Comparison articles cite 2023 numbers in 2026 dollars. This
post is the honest pricing landscape today.

## Who this is for

Operators in the evaluation phase trying to budget for an AI voice
agent. If you are already deployed, this is mostly orientation.

### Tier 0: Free + per-minute

Vendors like Goodcall, Synthflow's free tier, and similar entry-points.
Free monthly fee, then $0.10-$0.30 per minute of actual call time.
Looks cheap on the surface. The math degrades fast:

- 200 calls/month × 3 min average × $0.20/min = **$120/month**
- 500 calls/month × 3 min average × $0.20/min = **$300/month**
- 1,000 calls/month × 3 min average × $0.20/min = **$600/month**

By the time you have any real call volume, per-minute pricing costs
more than fixed monthly pricing on a comparable tier.

### Tier 1: Fixed monthly entry ($99-$299/month)

Smith.ai AI Voice, Vapi self-serve, Bland.ai standard. Fixed monthly
with limits — capped concurrent calls, capped monthly minutes, basic
integrations. Works for solo operators with simple call profiles.

Limits to watch:

- Concurrent call limits (often 1-3 simultaneous)
- Monthly minute caps that overage-bill
- Integration depth (usually webhook-only, no native CRM sync)
- Voice options (often only generic stock voices)

### Tier 2: Fixed monthly business ($297-$697/month)

Implenix, Ruby AI, RingCentral AI, others. The category sweet spot for
small-to-medium businesses with actual call volume. Includes:

- Unlimited concurrent calls
- Unlimited monthly minutes
- Industry-specific playbook
- Two-way CRM integration (HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, Zoho)
- Live calendar booking
- Live transfer rules
- 24/7 coverage included
- Implementation support during onboarding

This is where most legitimate business deployments land.

### Tier 3: Custom / enterprise ($1,000-$5,000+/month)

Multi-location operations, custom voice cloning, dedicated
implementation engineers, custom integrations, service-level
agreements. Scoped per deployment.

## The hidden costs that catch buyers off-guard

Five line items most vendors do not disclose upfront:

1. **Per-call overage fees.** "Unlimited" often has a soft cap.
   Always ask: "What happens at 5x my expected volume?"
2. **Integration setup fees.** $500-$2,500 one-time for non-template
   CRMs. Standard for HubSpot/Salesforce, custom-priced for niche
   systems.
3. **Per-location fees** for multi-location operations. Often $50-$150
   per additional location per month.
4. **Voice customization.** Custom voice cloning runs $500-$3,000
   one-time depending on quality tier.
5. **After-hours premium.** Some "fixed monthly" plans add a premium
   tier for after-hours coverage. Always confirm 24/7 is included.

## The ROI math that determines what tier to buy

The pricing question is the wrong question. The right question is:
*what tier produces the highest net ROI for my business?*

For most local-services and professional-services businesses with
100+ inbound calls/month, the Tier 2 ($297-$697/month) deployment
produces the strongest net ROI because:

- The price is fixed regardless of volume growth
- The integration depth captures the calls the AI handles
- The implementation support gets the deployment live correctly
- The 24/7 inclusion captures after-hours pipeline

The Tier 1 plans usually disappoint at any real volume because the
limits start hurting. The Tier 3 plans only make sense above 2,000
calls/month or multi-location complexity.

## How to compare honestly

Three-step framework for comparing AI agent vendors on price:

1. **Calculate your true total cost** at your expected volume after
   90 days, including overages, integration fees, and any premium
   tiers. Demand this number from every vendor.
2. **Compare against your missed-call cost.** Run the [audit](/audit).
   If your missed-call cost is 5x+ the monthly AI cost, the ROI is
   decisive regardless of which Tier 2 vendor you pick.
3. **Pick on implementation quality, not headline price.** The cheapest
   vendor with a bad implementation produces zero ROI. The slightly
   more expensive vendor with a working implementation produces all
   the value.
