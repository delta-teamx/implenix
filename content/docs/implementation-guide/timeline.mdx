# Implementation Timeline

Standard deployment: 7 to 14 business days. This page walks through what happens each day.

## Day 1, Kickoff and discovery call

**Duration:** 60 minutes.

Kickoff call with your deployment owner and any relevant team members. We confirm scope, walk through the timeline, and start discovery. You share access to recent call recordings.

**Output:** Signed scope document, initial call log delivered to us.

## Day 2, Recording review and vocabulary catalog

**Duration:** internal, on our side.

We listen to 20 to 40 of your recent inbound recordings. We catalog your callers' vocabulary, common intents, hidden intake patterns, and any red flags. We build the "hard-stop" list of things the agent should never do.

**Output:** Discovery findings document delivered to you for review.

## Day 3, Discovery review call

**Duration:** 45 minutes.

We walk through discovery findings with you. You correct any misreadings, add context, and approve the hard-stop list. This is the last chance to add or change scope before script build starts.

**Output:** Approved discovery findings, ready-to-build script requirements.

## Days 4 to 5, Draft script

**Duration:** internal.

We write the 7-section script covering all identified intent branches. First draft is delivered to you at end of day 5.

**Output:** Draft script for your review.

## Days 6 to 7, Script iteration

**Duration:** async collaborative.

You mark up the script. We revise. Usually 1 to 2 rounds before script is approved. Some deployments finish in one round; some need 3 or 4.

**Output:** Approved script ready for integration wiring.

## Days 8 to 9, Integration wiring

**Duration:** internal.

CRM connection (OAuth or API key setup). Calendar integration. SMS provisioning through Twilio. Transfer bridge configuration. Every integration tested with a live end-to-end write appearing in your source system.

**Output:** All integrations verified working. Shadow number provisioned.

## Days 10 to 11, Internal test battery

**Duration:** internal.

Our QA team runs 30 to 50 mock calls against the shadow number. All standard intents, edge cases, escalations, adversarial scenarios. Issues identified are fixed in-line.

**Output:** Test-battery report shared with you.

## Day 12, Client test window

**Duration:** you drive.

Shadow number is available for your team to test freely. Book test appointments, try edge cases, verify the CRM writes correctly. Report any issues to your deployment lead.

**Output:** Your issues logged for pre-cutover fixes.

## Day 13, Cutover

**Duration:** 30 minutes to 2 hours depending on porting.

Cutover happens. Options:

- **Forwarding.** Instant. Your existing number forwards to the agent. Reversible in under an hour if needed.
- **Full port.** Takes 5 to 10 business days from LOA signing to complete. Better for long-term but requires planning.

**Output:** Agent is live on your inbound.

## Day 14, Silent-monitor day 1

**Duration:** monitoring.

Our team silent-monitors 100 percent of calls. Any issue triggers immediate intervention. You get real-time updates on call volumes and outcomes.

**Output:** First 24-hour production report.

## Days 15 to 16, Silent-monitor days 2 to 3

Continued monitoring at reduced intensity. Any escalations get same-hour response. Standard production monitoring cadence starts on day 4.

## Regulated industry extension

For HIPAA, legal, or financial services deployments, add 7 to 14 days for compliance review cycles. Compliance touchpoints:

- Day 2 to 3: compliance officer or CCO joins discovery.
- Day 5: draft compliance review of script hard-stops.
- Day 9: integration compliance review (BAA verification, licensing gate check).
- Day 12: pre-cutover final compliance sign-off.

## Multi-location or complex integration extension

Add 3 to 7 days per additional location. Add 5 to 10 days for ServiceTitan, Epic, or other complex integrations.
