# Custom Webhook Integration

If your CRM or business system is not in our native integrations list, we integrate via HTTP webhook. This works for any system that can expose or consume HTTP endpoints.

## The two webhook directions

**Outbound webhook (agent to your system).** After every call, the agent posts structured call data to your endpoint. This is the standard pattern for creating leads, logging activities, or triggering downstream automation in a system we do not integrate with natively.

**Inbound webhook (your system to agent).** Your system posts to an Implenix endpoint to trigger an outbound call, update the agent's knowledge, or push context for an upcoming inbound. Less common but supported for advanced workflows.

## Outbound webhook payload

Standard JSON payload posted to your endpoint. Example fields:

- call_id (unique identifier)
- direction (inbound or outbound)
- from_number and to_number
- start_time, end_time, duration_seconds
- caller_name (if captured)
- caller_email (if captured)
- caller_address (if captured)
- intent (booking, inquiry, complaint, other)
- outcome (booked, transferred, escalated, resolved)
- appointment_details (if booked)
- transcript (full call transcript)
- audio_url (signed URL to recording)
- custom_fields (whatever we captured based on your intake script)

We customize the payload structure to match what your system expects.

## Authentication for your webhook

We support standard auth patterns on outbound webhooks:

- API key in header
- Bearer token
- HMAC signature verification (recommended for production)
- Basic auth

## Retry and error handling

If your endpoint returns a non-2xx response, the agent retries with exponential backoff up to 3 times. If all retries fail, the call is queued to a fallback log and your operations team gets an alert. We do not silently drop failed writes.

## Idempotency

Every webhook payload includes a unique call_id. If you retry processing on your side, using the call_id as an idempotency key prevents duplicate lead creation.

## Rate limiting

Standard rate is up to 10 requests per second per deployment. For higher throughput, we can adjust. Notify your endpoint owner if your infrastructure needs specific rate handling.

## Testing before go-live

We provide a test endpoint you can point to during development. Send yourself synthetic webhook payloads to verify your endpoint parses correctly and creates the right records on your side.

## Common patterns

**CRM proxy.** Your webhook receives the payload, translates to your CRM's API format, and posts to your CRM. Common when you want abstraction between us and your CRM.

**Data warehouse ingest.** Payload lands in your data warehouse for analytics. Common for enterprise deployments with BI needs.

**Multi-system fan-out.** Payload triggers writes to multiple systems (CRM, marketing automation, analytics). Your webhook handles the fan-out logic.

**Message queue.** Payload lands in Kafka, RabbitMQ, or SQS for asynchronous processing.
