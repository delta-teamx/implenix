The Implenix deployment process has four phases: **discovery**, **agent build**, **integration**, and **go-live**.

## Step 1 — Discovery call

We map your current call flow, top intents, and CRM integrations.

## Step 2 — Agent build

We assemble your call script, voice, and qualification logic.

## Step 3 — Integration

We connect to your CRM, calendar, and phone routing.

## Step 4 — Go-live

We test against your business in a staging window, then route real traffic.
