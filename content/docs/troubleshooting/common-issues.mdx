# Common Issues

Every production deployment encounters issues occasionally. Here are the most common ones and how to resolve them.

## Agent is not answering calls

**First check:** Is call forwarding still active on your existing carrier? Log into your carrier admin and verify the forwarding rule is in place with the correct Twilio destination.

**Second check:** Is your Twilio account funded? If auto-recharge is off and balance ran to zero, calls will not route. Check Twilio dashboard.

**Third check:** Is your agent's deployment status "active" in the Implenix admin dashboard? If not, contact support.

## Callers reporting agent hung up on them

**Common causes:**
- Extended silence from caller (agent has silence-tolerance limits).
- Agent misinterpreted an escalation trigger and transferred to a target that dropped.
- Network issue on the caller's end.

**How to investigate:** Pull the specific call recording from the dashboard. Listen to what happened just before the hangup. Report to support with call ID.

## Booking not appearing in calendar

**First check:** Is the calendar integration still authenticated? OAuth tokens can expire; re-authorization may be needed.

**Second check:** Did the booking write to the correct calendar? Multi-calendar setups sometimes have routing issues.

**Third check:** Is there a validation rule in your calendar system blocking the write? Check calendar app logs.

**Common cause:** Time zone mismatch. If the agent shows one time and calendar shows another, verify time zone configuration.

## Duplicate contacts in CRM

**Common cause:** Phone number fuzzy match not catching variations. "555-1234" and "(555) 123-4" are the same number but may not match without normalization.

**Resolution:** We can tune the match confidence threshold. Report to support with example duplicates.

## Calls transferring too often

**Common cause:** Escalation triggers too loose. Sensitivity detection may be firing on false positives.

**Resolution:** Monthly review of transfer patterns. We adjust thresholds during regular tuning cadence. For urgent adjustment, request via support.

## Calls not transferring when they should

**Common cause:** Escalation triggers too tight, or transfer target unavailable.

**Resolution:** Review the specific calls that should have transferred. Adjust rules or fallback logic. Verify transfer target phone numbers are current.

## Voice quality issues

**Common cause:** Network congestion on caller side, or codec mismatch on carrier side.

**Investigation:** Check if issue is with all callers or specific ones. If all, contact support for infrastructure check. If specific callers, likely a network issue on their side.

## Agent speaking wrong language

**Common cause:** Language detection misfired on unusual accent or phrasing.

**Resolution:** Verify language configuration for the deployment. Add explicit language override rules if needed.

## Custom fields not populating in CRM

**Common cause:** Field name mismatch or write permission missing.

**Resolution:** Verify field internal name matches what the agent expects. Check integration user has write access to the field.

## Reporting numbers seem off

**Common cause:** Reporting time zone vs your local time zone mismatch, or reporting filter excluding certain call types.

**Resolution:** Verify reporting filters. Cross-reference with CRM records for specific date range.

## Emergency

For active production issues affecting call handling (agent completely down, wrong routing on high-stakes calls), contact support directly at the priority support number provided in your deployment package. Standard response time: 30 minutes during business hours, 2 hours after hours.
