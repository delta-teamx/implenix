# Voicemail Handling

Most Implenix deployments replace voicemail entirely, the agent answers and captures the intake instead of playing a message. But some scenarios still call for voicemail behavior. Here is how those cases are handled.

## When voicemail behavior applies

**Explicit request.** Some callers say "I want to leave a voicemail" or "just take a message." The agent complies, takes the structured message, and delivers it through the standard channel.

**Fallback on tool failure.** If the calendar or CRM is completely down and the agent cannot book, it takes a structured message with promised callback window.

**Fallback on human unavailable.** If a call needs to transfer but no human is available, the message-taking behavior kicks in.

**Configured hours.** Some deployments configure the agent to take messages only during certain windows, and to book actively during others.

## What a structured message includes

Unlike traditional voicemail, agent-taken messages have structured fields:

- Caller name
- Callback number (verified against caller ID)
- Reason for call in the caller's own words
- Urgency level (asked explicitly)
- Best callback time
- Any specific person requested

The message posts to your CRM as an activity and optionally triggers an alert to the appropriate team member.

## Voicemail replacement pattern

For most deployments, the agent replaces voicemail entirely. Common transition steps:

**Week 1 to 2 post-cutover.** Traditional voicemail box remains active as a safety net. Any callers who reach it (rare, but possible if the agent has an outage) still leave messages.

**Week 3 onwards.** Voicemail box disabled. All inbound goes through the agent. Message-taking behavior kicks in for the specific scenarios above.

## Traditional voicemail box coexistence

If you want to keep a traditional voicemail box alongside the agent (some businesses do for specific extensions or after-hours emergency), we configure the routing accordingly:

- Main line to agent.
- Direct-dial extensions to human with voicemail fallback.
- After-hours specific voicemail for emergency line if you want a low-tech fallback.

## Voicemail retrieval

Structured messages appear in:

- Your CRM as activity or task.
- Your email inbox with transcript and audio if configured.
- Your SMS if configured.

Standard practice: main deployment lead gets email + SMS for all messages, appropriate team members get email only for their assigned messages.

## Common issues

**Messages not appearing.** Check delivery channel configuration. Verify email/SMS destination is correct.

**Duplicate messages.** Sometimes callers leave messages and also book; the intake creates both. Not usually a problem, verify with your team's workflow.

**Long messages.** Callers occasionally give very long descriptions. The agent transcribes accurately; the intake captures the structured fields, and the full transcript is available for review.

**Callback promises.** The agent promises specific callback windows. Missing those windows damages trust. Ensure the promised windows match what your team can actually deliver.
